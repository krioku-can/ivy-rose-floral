🐾 Pet-Friendly Arrangements

Beautiful Blooms, Safe for Your Best Friend

You shouldn't have to choose between fresh flowers and your pet's safety. Our pet-friendly arrangements use only non-toxic, vet-approved blooms — so your home stays beautiful and safe.

Why Pet-Friendly?

Many Popular Flowers Are Toxic to Pets

⚠️ Common flowers that are toxic to cats & dogs:

Toxic to Cats

  • Lilies (extremely dangerous)
  • Tulips
  • Daffodils
  • Chrysanthemums
  • Hyacinths
  • Amaryllis

Toxic to Dogs

  • Tulips
  • Daffodils
  • Hyacinths
  • Oleander
  • Autumn Crocus
  • Amaryllis

Source: ASPCA Animal Poison Control. When in doubt, always check ASPCA's plant database.

Our Process

How We Keep Your Pets Safe

1

Every Bloom Vetted

We cross-reference every flower against the ASPCA toxic plant database. If it's not safe, it doesn't go in the arrangement — period.

2

Clearly Labeled

Every pet-friendly arrangement comes with a card listing every bloom used and a confirmation that it's non-toxic for cats and dogs.

3

Delivered with Care

Hand-delivered to your door, just like all our arrangements. No worry, no guesswork — just beautiful, safe flowers.
