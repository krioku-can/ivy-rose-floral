Our Story

Grown with Intention, Arranged with Heart

Ivy & Rose Floral Co. started with a simple belief: flowers should feel personal. Every bouquet is handcrafted using seasonal blooms sourced from local growers and our own garden.

Based in Hamilton, Ohio, we specialize in weddings, events, and thoughtful everyday arrangements. No mass production. No assembly lines. Just beautiful flowers, arranged one stem at a time.
